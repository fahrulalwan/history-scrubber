History scrubber

Keep chosen sites out of your browser history, without logging out of them.

1

How it works

Adding a site deletes the history it already has. After that, each visit is deleted the next time you close a tab. Anything missed, after a crash for example, goes at the next Chrome start.

Deleted

  • Pages on your sites, including redirect hops
  • Searches for the site's name
  • Download-list entries
  • Your Google account's copy, if history sync is on

Stays

  • Cookies, so you stay logged in
  • Downloaded files
  • Bookmarks and saved passwords

To do this it reads your browsing history. Everything stays on this computer: no servers, no analytics, no network calls of its own.

2

Add your sites

The list starts empty, and nothing is deleted until you add a site. Type a domain; subdomains are included.

Later, add the site you're on straight from the toolbar button.

3

Pin it to your toolbar

The button shows whether the site you're on is on your list, and adds or removes it in one click.

Click the puzzle-piece icon at the top right of Chrome, then the pin next to History scrubber.
