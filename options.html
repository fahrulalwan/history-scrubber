History scrubber

Deletes history for these sites when you add one, when you close a tab, and again when Chrome starts. Cookies stay, so you stay logged in.

Sites

Incognito: links to that site open in an incognito window, so no history is written at all. Incognito has its own cookies, so you'll be logged out there.

Deleted

  • Pages on these sites, including redirect hops
  • Searches for the site's name
  • Download-list entries
  • Your Google account's copy, through history sync

Stays

  • Cookies and logins
  • Downloaded files
  • Bookmarks and saved passwords
